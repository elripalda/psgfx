PSGFX 2.0
Custom icons and backgrounds for your PS5 homebrew and games, and your home-screen layout.

BEFORE YOU START
 - PS5 Upload's payload must be running on your PS5 (the same setup you use to upload homebrew).
 - The PS5 Upload app (version 6 or newer) must be open on this computer and connected
   to your PS5. PSGFX does its work through it.
 - Your computer and PS5 must be on the same network.

STEPS
 1. Windows: double-click PSGFX.exe; PSGFX opens in your browser.
    Mac: open PSGFX.app; PSGFX opens its own window.
    (Windows: if SmartScreen warns, click "More info" > "Run anyway"; if the firewall asks,
     allow it on private networks.
     Mac: if macOS says it can't verify PSGFX, open System Settings > Privacy & Security
     (macOS 12 and older: System Preferences > Security & Privacy > General),
     click "Open Anyway", then open PSGFX again.)
 2. PSGFX asks for your PS5's IP address. Type it and click Connect.
    (Next time it connects by itself. Click the connection button at the top to change it.)
 3. Pick a homebrew app or an installed game from the list.
 4. Drop an image on the preview's tile (icon) or background (background),
    or use the "Choose image" buttons. Any PNG or JPEG works.
 5. Close that app or game on the PS5, then click "Apply to PS5".
 6. Restart the PS5 to see the new art.

COMMUNITY ICONS (new in 2.0)
 - Pick an app or game: the Icon tab shows icons other people shared for it.
   Click one to use it, then Apply to PS5.
 - To share yours, choose an icon, click "Share your icon" and accept the guidelines.

MUSIC (new in 1.3)
 - Drop an MP3, WAV or FLAC on the preview, or use "Choose song".
 - Set where it starts and how long it runs, listen to it, then Apply.
 - It plays when the app's or game's tile is selected. Restart the PS5 to hear it.

UNDO
 - Select the app and click "Restore original".

HOME LAYOUT (new in 1.1)
 1. Connect, then click "Home layout" at the top.
 2. Select a tile: choose Games, Media or Hidden, or rename it.
 3. Drag games into order or use A-Z / Z-A. Tick "Lock order" to keep it.
 4. Click "Apply to PS5" (optionally save it as a preset), then restart the PS5.
 - Backups and presets live in the "PSGFX Layout" folder next to PSGFX.exe (Mac: in ~/Library/Application Support/PSGFX).
   Use "Backups" to undo, or Safe Mode > Rebuild Database as a last resort.
 - After a firmware update, open "Presets", load your layout and apply it again.

TIPS
 - Best results: icons 512x512 or larger, backgrounds 3840x2160.
 - A game update may bring back that game's original art. Just apply yours again.
 - Your PS5 IP is saved in psgfx.json next to PSGFX.exe (Mac: in ~/Library/Application Support/PSGFX).
 - To quit: Windows, close the black console window; Mac, close the PSGFX window or press Cmd-Q.
